### What does FreezeRadar do?

FreezeRadar checks wallet addresses for issuer blacklist or freezeable-asset status involving USDT, USDC, PAXG, and XAUt, and surfaces sanctions-related exposure and live freeze/release activity. It provides free public checks for investigators, OTC desks, exchanges, and compliance teams.

**The lowdown:** It’s a wallet-screening and stablecoin-freeze investigation tool.

### Use in Reporting

FreezeRadar can be used to:

* Check cryptocurrency addresses against supported sanctions sources.
* Identify direct issuer-blacklist or freeze-related exposure.
* Investigate the counterparties associated with a wallet.
* Trace the apparent sources of funds entering a wallet.
* Identify behavioural patterns such as structuring and peel-chain activity.
* Investigate exposure to mixers, darknet markets, scams and other labelled entities.
* Document historical freeze, unfreeze and burn events.
* Compare wallet activity against official sanctions and enforcement information.
* Preserve wallet-risk findings as part of an investigative evidence trail.
* Generate shareable scan reports and exports where the relevant plan permits.
* Support investigations into suspicious crypto payments, stablecoin freezes and sanctions exposure.
* Provide leads for deeper blockchain analysis using transaction hashes and related addresses.

**Important note:** Make sure to cite the checked address, network, asset, result, and timestamp, and verify material conclusions against issuer announcements and on-chain evidence.

### Summary

FreezeRadar is designed to identify sanctions exposure, issuer blacklist status, risky counterparties, funding-source patterns and other indicators associated with freezable assets. It sits best in the collection, verification, and analysis stages of the OSINT workflow and is most useful for initial blockchain-investigation and compliance triage.

### Ethical Considerations

• Treat outputs as investigative leads, not definitive accusations.

• Minimise personal data and avoid unnecessary wallet-address sharing.

• Corroborate sanctions or blacklist indicators with authoritative sources.

• Record timestamps because status can change.

• Follow applicable laws, due-process requirements, and internal review policies before taking action.
