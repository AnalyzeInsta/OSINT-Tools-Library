### What does Nations Online do?

Nations Online is a long-running country and geography reference site that brings together maps, country profiles, geographic facts, international codes and links to local and official information sources.

The site includes country information such as official and alternative country names, capitals, major cities, populations, languages, currencies, calling codes and country codes. It also provides political, administrative, physical and regional maps.

It’s stated that much of the information is compiled from national governments, ministries, embassies, statistical offices, local newspapers, NGOs and international organisations, with additional material from sources such as the World Factbook and WTO.

**The lowdown:** It’s ideal as a foundational tool and for quickly establishing geographic and country-context information and finding relevant local or official sources.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

Nations Online can be useful for:

* Establishing the geographic location and regional context of an investigation.
* Checking country names, alternative names, capitals and international country codes.
* Using political, administrative and physical maps to understand borders, neighbouring states and terrain.
* Identifying official government, embassy, statistical and other local sources linked from country profiles.
* Finding local newspapers and other country-specific information sources for further research.
* Supporting background research on countries, territories, cities, languages, currencies and administrative structures.
* Providing contextual reference material that can help investigators formulate searches and identify appropriate primary sources.

As an example of real-world use, [a military educational case study](https://www.mwcoe.org/docs/lesson-learned-2024-workshop-summary/04-2008-russo-georgia-war-case-study/) credits Nations Online for maps showing controlled territory and Russian force axes during the 2008 Russo-Georgia war.&#x20;

| **Capabilities**                                                                                  | **Limitations**                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Country profiles covering geography, capitals, languages, codes and other reference information.  | Information is compiled from many sources and may not always be current.                                       |
| Political, administrative, physical and regional maps.                                            | Maps are reference products and should not automatically be treated as authoritative boundary evidence.        |
| Alternative and historical country names and country codes.                                       | Naming and territorial classifications can vary between sources and jurisdictions.                             |
| Links to government, statistical, media and other country-specific sources.                       | Linked material varies in quality and should be assessed independently.                                        |
| Broad geographic coverage in one searchable resource.                                             | It’s primarily a reference index, not a specialist investigative database or real-time intelligence platform.  |

### Summary

Nations Online fits primarily into the discovery and verification stages of the OSINT workflow, with its main value in providing a quick way to establish where something is, how a place is named, what its geographic context is, and where to find more authoritative country-specific information.&#x20;

However, it’s best treated as a reference and discovery resource and where a geographic, demographic, political or administrative fact is important to a report, you should cite the underlying official/specialist source where available.&#x20;

### Ownership

The [site's terms ](http://nationsonline.org/oneworld/disclaimer.htm)identify Klaus Kästle as the publisher and editor-in-chief of the Nations Online Project. A [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/klaus-k%C3%A4stle-975b2a42/) identifies him as residing in Bangkok, Thailand.&#x20;

The project states that it began in early 1998 as a reference work for netscout.net and subsequently developed into a broader guide to countries, cultures and geographic information.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat Nations Online as a secondary reference source where a primary government, international organisation or specialist source is available.
* Check the date and provenance of important statistics or geographic claims.
* Be cautious with disputed borders, territories and politically sensitive geographical classifications.
* Respect the copyright and licensing conditions attached to individual maps and images as Nations Online states that its images and artwork come from various sources and may carry different licences.
