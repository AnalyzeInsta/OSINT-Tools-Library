### What does WildEye do?

WildEye brings together information about wildlife crime and other environmental offences in searchable, interactive maps. Its records can include the commodities involved, seizures, arrests, court proceedings, convictions, penalties and other case details.

The platform has regional versions covering global incidents, Europe, Asia, Southern Africa and Eastern Africa. It is designed to make scattered information easier to find and compare, particularly for journalists investigating illegal wildlife trade and related environmental crime.

**The lowdown:** It’s a geospatial research and case-tracking tool for wildlife trafficking and environmental crime.&#x20;

### Use in Reporting

WildEye can be useful for:

* Identifying reported wildlife-trafficking incidents involving particular species, animal products, timber or other commodities.
* Mapping seizures and arrests across countries and regions.
* Tracking whether cases progress from arrest or prosecution to conviction.
* Comparing enforcement activity across jurisdictions and identifying apparent gaps in case outcomes.
* Finding cases for follow-up through court records, official statements and freedom-of-information requests.
* Investigating possible cross-border trafficking patterns and recurring commodities.
* Generating leads for research into wildlife-crime networks and environmental offences.
* Monitoring selected cases or areas through email alerts.
* Supporting data-driven environmental journalism and cross-border investigations.

You can view Oxpeckers data and investigations [here.](https://oxpeckers.org/get-the-data/)

### Summary

WildEye is a free environmental-crime platform for mapping and researching wildlife trafficking and related offences. Its strongest use is in the discovery and collection stages of an investigation, followed by verification and analysis. It helps researchers find incidents, compare enforcement outcomes and develop leads for reporting, but it should not replace primary court records or independent corroboration.

### Ownership

WildEye is a project of Oxpeckers Investigative Environmental Journalism, an African non-profit organisation focused on investigative environmental reporting, data analysis and geospatial storytelling. It was founded and is headed by [Fiona Macleod.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fiona-macleod-275250a/)

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat a seizure, arrest or allegation as a reported event, not proof of guilt.
* Verify names, charges, outcomes and penalties against original legal or official records.
* Do not assume that a concentration of recorded incidents proves a higher underlying crime rate. It may also reflect differences in enforcement, reporting or data availability.
* Avoid identifying people beyond what is justified by reliable public-interest evidence.
* Follow applicable laws and data-use terms when reusing datasets, and protect sensitive information that could endanger people, wildlife or ongoing investigations.
