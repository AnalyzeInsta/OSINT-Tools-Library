### What does Vigil Consilium do?

Vigil Consilium is a structured geopolitical research and OSINT analysis platform. It maintains interconnected, source-backed records across conflict and security systems, linking developments to actors, relationships, military structures, geography, logistics, economic conditions, recorded activity and standing analytical questions.

Rather than acting primarily as a raw-data discovery or breaking-news tool, Vigil helps researchers contextualise information, trace what an assessment rests on, follow changes over time, and identify uncertainty or evidence gaps.

Public Vigil includes Read, Explore, Map and Watch, alongside Weekly Briefs, Analytical Notes and Force Atlas. Records carry their sources, dates, confidence and review status where applicable. An optional Professional workspace adds deeper evidence tracing, monitoring, comparison, conditional analysis and briefing tools over the same maintained research corpus.

**The lowdown:** Rather than simply presenting individual news stories, it attempts to preserve the wider system around them.

### Use in Reporting

Vigil can be used to contextualise geopolitical, defence and conflict reporting by connecting individual developments to the wider systems around them.

**Typical uses include:**

* Tracing actors, relationships and military structures;
* Checking how an event fits into a longer timeline;
* Examining logistics, infrastructure and geographic dependencies;
* Comparing activity over time through recorded Map/Tempo series;
* Identifying standing analytical questions and the evidence that would change an assessment;
* Following source links, confidence grades and review dates back to the underlying evidence.

Vigil has also been used as a cited source in external reporting. On 28 September 2026, the [Caspian Policy Center](https://caspianpolicy.org/research/weekly-media-highlights/headlines-from-the-caspian-september-28-2026) cited Vigil Consilium alongside RFE/RL in its Headlines from the Caspian roundup for coverage of Armenia’s Constitutional Court clearing the framework agreement underpinning the TRIPP corridor.

| Connected actor, relationship and event records.                    | Curated rather than comprehensive or real-time.                               |

### Summary

Vigil Consilium is most useful in the analysis, contextualisation and monitoring stages of an OSINT workflow and is best used after information has been found, to understand how it fits into the wider system, what changed, and what remains uncertain.

Its main strength is connecting sourced developments to actors, relationships, force structures, geography, logistics and standing analytical questions, while preserving dates, confidence and uncertainty.&#x20;

It is less suited to raw-data discovery or real-time collection, and its coverage is limited to defined security areas rather than global events.

### Ownership

Independent, founder-led research project maintained by Vigil Consilium and based in Europe.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Follow and verify the underlying cited sources when using Vigil in published work.
* Preserve stated confidence levels, uncertainty and information gaps when repeating assessments.
* Do not treat approximate or historical map locations as live operational information.
* Vigil intentionally excludes current positions, movements and targeting-relevant detail.
* Public research does not require an account or personal information.
* Avoid presenting Vigil assessments as definitive where the underlying record is contested or incomplete.
