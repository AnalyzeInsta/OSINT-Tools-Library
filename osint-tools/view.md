### What does VIEW do?

VIEW is an independent directory of publicly accessible Telegram channels, groups and bots. Users can search titles, original descriptions, AI-generated descriptions and usernames, or browse topic categories and public collections.&#x20;

Result cards and detail pages give descriptions, resource type, language and scores. Chinese, English and Russian interfaces are available. These functions can help build a preliminary source shortlist but they do not establish that a source or claim is authentic.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s ideal for discovering, comparing and organising public Telegram channels, groups and bots relevant to an investigation or research topic.&#x20;

### How to Use:

**1. Open https://view.tg/en in a browser without logging in. Click Search and enter a relevant keyword or username.**&#x20;

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (659).png" alt="" height="409" width="602">

**2. Use All, Channel, Group or Bot to narrow the resource type. Search can match descriptions as well as names, so inspect each result rather than assuming an exact match.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (660).png" alt="" height="150" width="412">

**3. Open a candidate detail page and review its description, language, category and original Telegram reference. Treat generated summaries and scores as leads.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (661).png" alt="" height="223" width="602">

**4. Verify the source directly in Telegram and corroborate any factual claim elsewhere before reporting. Preserve evidence using an appropriate separate workflow; this directory is not an evidentiary archive.**

**Note:** Review cookie settings and the privacy policy before entering a sensitive research query.

### Use in Reporting

VIEW can be useful for:

* Discovering public Telegram channels, groups and bots relevant to a subject or investigation.
* Finding specialist, regional or language-specific Telegram communities that may be difficult to identify through ordinary web searching.
* Comparing descriptions, categories, languages and audience snapshots across multiple public resources.
* Identifying related resources and building a list of Telegram sources for ongoing monitoring.
* Providing a convenient starting point for finding the original Telegram channel, group or bot behind a directory entry.
* Supporting background research and investigative lead generation around public Telegram ecosystems.

**Potential workflow:** identify candidate public Telegram news channels or topical communities, note the original source links, then independently check authorship, timing, context and corroborating evidence.&#x20;

### Summary

VIEW is useful as a supplementary discovery-stage directory when locating public Telegram resources across languages. Its category and description layers can help narrow a shortlist, but automated summaries, mixed/adult content, query recording and uncertain coverage mean it should not be used alone for verification or evidentiary claims. Editorial review and independent testing are requested.

### Ethical Considerations

* Use only for lawful research with a legitimate purpose; do not use public listings to harass, stalk or target people.
* Public availability does not eliminate privacy rights; minimize personal information in notes and publication.
* Expect adult and sensitive-topic material, including in broad results, and avoid unnecessary exposure or redistribution.
* Check original sources and corroborate claims; do not repeat automated characterizations as established facts.
* Consider search logging and third-party processing before entering sensitive queries
* Respect Telegram and destination-service rules, copyright and local law.
