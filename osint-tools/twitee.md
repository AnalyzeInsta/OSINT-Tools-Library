### What does Twitee do?

Twitee provides a browser-based way to access publicly available X content (public profiles, posts, available replies, search results, and media) without requiring you to sign into X. It supports account and date filters, Top and Latest search results, country trends, and downloads of available images and videos.

For standard public X profile and post URLs, Twitee supports a direct-link shortcut: replace x.com with twitee.co and keep the path unchanged.

**The lowdown:** It’s particularly useful for researching public usernames, finding relevant posts and conversations, examining public media and documenting information available from X without directly signing into the platform.

### How to Use:

**1. For a standard public X profile or post URL, replace x.com with twitee.co and keep the path unchanged.**&#x20;

**Examples:**

Profile: [https://x.com/NASA](https://x.com/NASA) → [https://twitee.co/NASA](https://twitee.co/NASA)&#x20;

Post: [https://x.com/NASA/status/1951245359604203914](https://x.com/NASA/status/1951245359604203914)&#x20;

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (668).png" alt="" height="573" width="602">

**2. For a profile, use https://twitee.co/twitter-viewer, enter a public @username or profile link, and select View profile.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (669).png" alt="" height="528" width="602">

**3. For a single post, use https://twitee.co/tweet-viewer. Paste its public URL or numeric post ID and select View post to read the post and available replies.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (670).png" alt="" height="308" width="602">

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (672).png" alt="" height="167" width="602">

**4. To find posts, use https://twitee.co/twitter-search. Enter a keyword or hashtag, narrow by account or date, and choose Top or Latest. The Before date excludes that day.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (673).png" alt="" height="329" width="602">

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/unknown (674).png" alt="" height="304" width="602">

**5. To save attached media, open the video or image downloader, paste the public post link, and select an available file.**

**Note:** Be careful of popups/third-party adverts.

**Reporting Note:** Keep the original post URL and retrieval time with your research notes. Check important findings against the original source.

### Use in Reporting

Twitee can be used for the following:

* **Public statements:** inspect a post and its replies before quoting an individual or organisation.
* **Account research:** compare a public handle, bio, and recent activity with other sources.
* **Event research:** narrow posts by keyword, account, and date to find material relevant to a developing story.
* **Visual research:** review available photos or videos before separate geolocation or authenticity checks.

| **Capabilities**                                                                                                              | **Limitations**                                                                                                                                           |

| Keyword and hashtag search with account, date, language, and media filters.                                                   | Availability of media can change if the original content is removed or becomes unavailable.                                                               |

### Summary

Twitee is a free, independent X/Twitter OSINT viewer and search tool that allows investigators to browse public profiles, posts, replies, media and trends without requiring an X account. It supports discovery and initial collection by helping investigators find public source material and retain source links for verification.

### Ownership

Twitee is independently operated by [Tommy De](https://www.indiehackers.com/tommy_twitee) and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X.&#x20;

### Ethical Considerations

* Respect privacy and consider harm before publishing identifying details.
* Keep source URLs, dates, and context with quoted posts or saved media.
* Corroborate claims and distinguish an account's statements from verified facts.
* Download or reuse media only with the necessary rights or permission.
* Do not use the service for harassment, access-control bypasses, or abusive automated traffic.
