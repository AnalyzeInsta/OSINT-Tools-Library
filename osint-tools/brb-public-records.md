### What does BRB Public Records do?

BRB Public Records acts as a research directory for US public records. Its free resources are organised by state and point researchers toward county, city and state record systems. Individual jurisdiction pages can identify courts, record offices, addresses, websites, telephone numbers, record types and restrictions.&#x20;

**The lowdown:** It’s a US public-records locator and research reference tool.

### Cookies:&#x20;

The site uses first-party session cookies including sessionCreated, which records the session creation time, and sessionId, which identifies the current browser session. It also uses warningModalPopped to retain temporary interface state. Google-domain cookies (SID, SIDCC, SOCS and SSID) were also observed during browsing, but these belong to Google's infrastructure rather than being first-party BRB cookies.

### Use in Reporting

BRB Public Records can be useful for:

* Locating the official government agency that holds a particular public record.
* Identifying the appropriate county, city or state jurisdiction for a records search.
* Finding court, property, recorded-document and other public-record sources.
* Determining whether records are available online, by telephone, by mail or through another access method.
* Identifying record-access restrictions and requirements before making a request.
* Finding information about applicable search, copying, certification or expedited-service fees.
* Tracing property, recorded-document, lien and judgment records through the relevant government offices.
* Building a research plan before conducting more detailed public-record searches.

BRB itself notes that recorded documents, judgments and liens can provide information about ownership, collateral and asset relationships, making these records useful starting points for asset and due-diligence research.

| **Capabilities**                                                                  | **Limitations**                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Covers public-record sources across US states, counties and cities.               | It’s primarily a directory/research guide rather than the underlying government record repository.                |
| Provides information about courts, agencies, record types and access routes.      | Coverage and availability depend on the underlying government agency.                                             |
| Identifies access methods, restrictions and fees for many record sources.         | A listed record source may still require payment, an account, an in-person request or other access requirements.  |
| Helps identify the correct jurisdiction and agency.                               | Free government searches may be incomplete or less comprehensive than onsite or paid searches.                    |
| Paid PRRS expands the research information to 28,000+ agencies and institutions.  | Subscription information is not the same as access to the underlying records themselves.                          |

### Summary

BRB Public Record’s free directory is particularly useful during the discovery and collection stages of an OSINT investigation, helping identify the correct jurisdiction, record type and primary source.&#x20;

The paid PRRS adds much more detailed information about access procedures, restrictions, fees and search requirements across more than 28,000 agencies.

### Ethical Considerations

* Treat BRB as a locator and research aid, not automatically as the authoritative source of the underlying record.
* Verify significant findings against the original government record.
* Check that the jurisdiction, court, agency and record dates actually correspond to the subject being investigated.
* Be careful with common names and possible false matches.
* Do not assume that the existence of a public record establishes wrongdoing.
* Avoid unnecessarily collecting or publishing sensitive personal information.
